## Methodology

Due to the noise in the data and properties of the materials, it is hard to find a "linear region" in the strain-stress figure.

To solve this, we adopted "sliding windows" method.

1. Select 0% to 60% of tensile strength as the "possible linear region", suppose there are $n$ data points inside this region.
2. From data point $1$ to data point $n-30$, pick it and its subsequent $29$ data points as the sliding window.
3. Carry out linear regression, record the slope $E$ and coefficient of determination $R^2$.
4. Plot $E-\epsilon$ and $R^2-\epsilon$ figure
5. The region with flat $E-\epsilon$ trend and high $R^2$ is the linear region (elastic region).

Then, plot the $\sigma$-$\epsilon$ graph and line $\sigma = E \times (\epsilon-0.002) + m$.
Find their intersection, yield strength and yield strain is obtained.
